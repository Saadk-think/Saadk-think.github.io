This Privacy Policy explains how Think Technology LLC, operating as Think Technologies, collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information. Think Technology LLC also operates The US Publisher, its publishing-services brand. This Policy applies to our websites, contact forms, communications, and services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, mobile number, postal address, and other contact details.
  • Inquiry details, consultation requests, quotations, project information, manuscripts, and files you provide.
  • Payment confirmations and transaction details supplied by payment processors; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Your separate choices regarding informational and marketing SMS messages.
  • Consent records, including submission time, the form used, IP address, and browser or device information.
  • Standard website usage, security, and diagnostic information.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, schedule consultations, and provide customer support.
  • Deliver contracted services and communicate about project status.
  • Send informational SMS messages when you have selected informational consent.
  • Send promotional SMS messages when you have separately selected marketing consent.
  • Maintain consent and opt-out records, protect our services, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Mobile information and SMS consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the text messaging services used to deliver the messages you requested.

We do not sell mobile numbers or SMS consent. Informational and marketing SMS choices are optional and separate. You may withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us using the details below.

4. Service providers and limited sharing

We may provide limited information to service providers that help us operate our business, such as hosting, payment processing, email, customer support, security, analytics, and text-message delivery providers. They may process information only to perform services for us or as required by law. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a lawful business transaction.

All sharing categories described above exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; that information will not be shared with third parties except aggregators and providers of the text-message services.

5. Manuscript and project confidentiality

Manuscripts, drafts, business materials, and other project files are treated as confidential and are made available only to personnel and service providers who need them to deliver the requested work, subject to appropriate duties of confidentiality.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and consent records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online transmission or storage method is completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. You may opt out of marketing email using the unsubscribe method provided and opt out of SMS by replying STOP. Submit privacy requests to info@thinktechnologies.net.

8. International processing

We operate through a remote team and may process information in the United States, Türkiye, or other locations where our service providers operate. We take reasonable steps to protect information consistent with this Policy.

9. Children

Our services are intended for adults and business users. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided information may contact us to request its removal.

10. Policy changes

We may update this Policy by publishing the revised version and changing the “Last updated” date. Material changes will apply prospectively as required by law.
